# Pharmaceutical Quality Control Internship
## Task 01 – Specification and Certificate of Analysis (COA) Reading

**Internship:** UpStartInterns – Pharmaceutical Quality Control  
**Project:** Specification and COA Review of Sucrose Extrapure  
**Product:** Sucrose Extrapure, 500 GM  
**Manufacturer:** Actylis  
**Product Code:** 11746SG500  
**Batch Number:** 961820225CZ  

---

### 1. Project Overview

This project is an educational exercise undertaken as part of the UpStartInterns Pharmaceutical Quality Control Internship.

The objective is to understand how product specifications and a Certificate of Analysis (COA) are interpreted during a basic quality-control review. A publicly available manufacturer's COA for Sucrose Extrapure was selected as the reference document.

### 2. Objectives

- Understand the purpose and structure of a Certificate of Analysis.
- Compare reported test results against the corresponding specifications.
- Identify conforming and potentially non-conforming results.
- Understand the distinction between out-of-specification (OOS) and out-of-trend (OOT) observations.
- Formulate a reasoned documentary quality assessment.

### 4. Methodology

The manufacturer's publicly available COA was reviewed. Each reported test result was compared with its corresponding acceptance criterion. The results were classified as Pass or Fail based on the information stated in the COA.

### 5. Summary of COA Assessment

| Test | Specification | Reported result | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Description | Colourless crystals/crystalline powder | Colourless crystalline powder | Pass |
| Solubility | 10% solution in water should be clear | Complies | Pass |
| Specific optical rotation | +66.4° to +66.6° | +66.5° | Pass |
| Residue on ignition (as SO₄) | Maximum 0.05% | 0.02% | Pass |
| Heavy metals (as Pb) | Maximum 0.0005% | Less than 0.0005% | Pass |
| Reducing sugars | Passes test | Complies | Pass |

### 6. OOS and OOT Assessment

**Out-of-Specification (OOS):** No OOS result was identified among the six tests reported in the reviewed COA.

**Out-of-Trend (OOT):** OOT status cannot be conclusively assessed from a single batch COA. Historical batch results, trend data and established statistical control limits were not available for comparison.

### 7. Overall Documentary Assessment

Based on the six reported tests and their stated acceptance criteria, the reviewed batch conforms to the specifications listed in the available manufacturer's COA.

This is a documentary assessment only. It does not represent independent laboratory testing, receiving-site quality control, or formal batch-release authorisation.

### 8. Conclusion

The exercise provided practical exposure to reading a Certificate of Analysis, interpreting acceptance criteria, comparing analytical results and understanding basic quality-control decision-making.

All six reported results met their corresponding specifications. No OOS result was identified. However, additional historical data would be required for a meaningful OOT assessment.
